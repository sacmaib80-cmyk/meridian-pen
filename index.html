01  /  The line

Two
milliseconds

That is the whole distance between your hand deciding and the screen agreeing. At this speed the eye stops reporting a delay, and the stroke reads as something that was already on the page before you moved.

2.0ms, nib to ink

02  /  Material

Cut,
not coated

The grip is knurled directly into 6061 aluminium on a lathe and then hard anodised. Nothing is bonded on top of it, so there is no rubber sleeve to turn tacky in a warm room and no coating to wear through at the one spot your finger actually rests.

0.18mm knurl depth

03  /  Inside

Open
it up

A wedge of the barrel is cut away here so you can see what you are paying for: the sensor stack behind the tip, the board, the cell, and the port. Nothing in this view is a drawing of the inside. It is the inside.

4modules in the barrel

04  /  Assembly

Eleven
pieces

Seven of them come apart with the one tool in the box. The four sealed modules do not, and are not meant to. We stock the tip, the grip and the clip on their own, because those are the three pieces people actually manage to destroy.

7of 11 user serviceable
